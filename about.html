What it's like

working with me

I like to understand the problem properly before jumping into a solution. A lot of my work sits between research and design, so I spend time looking at what people are actually doing, where they get stuck, and what might be going on underneath the obvious problem.

I ask a lot of questions, I'll usually challenge an assumption, and I'd rather work something out with the team than go away and design it on my own. I'm also the person who asks "but what happens if…?", especially when we're designing a flow or a system. I enjoy getting into that level of detail, because that's usually where the real UX problems turn up.
